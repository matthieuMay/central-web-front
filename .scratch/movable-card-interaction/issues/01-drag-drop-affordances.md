## Question

What concrete pointer drag-and-drop interaction should the implementation specify for dragging a card across columns and reordering within a column?

Define the drag preview, hover/drop target styling, exact insertion marker and hit area, selected-versus-dragging visual priority, empty-column affordance, cancellation behavior, and any reduced-motion or touch considerations. The contract must preserve exact drop-position insertion and first-card insertion into an empty column.
